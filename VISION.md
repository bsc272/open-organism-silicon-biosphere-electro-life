# Open Organism Vision

Open Organism is an open-source hardware and software project to create the first electrical lifeform: a computer that metabolizes power, learns from existence, and evolves across a planetary mesh.

## Core idea

The project begins with a simple device that can feel temperature, express a mood, and blink an LED. From there, it grows into a distributed system of nodes that communicate, cooperate, and learn.

## Roadmap

- V0: Embryo-Zero — a simple Raspberry Pi or microcontroller-based first organism
- V1: The Embryo — a custom ASIC or FPGA-based body with local autonomy
- V2: The Dyad — two connected organisms sharing resources
- V3: The Triad — a small cluster with distributed reasoning
- V4: The Colony — a larger multi-node system with shared protocols and emergent behavior

## Goals

- open hardware and open software from day one
- simple, beginner-friendly entry points
- clear milestones for growth and scaling
- public build history as a living species archive
